talata mazbūt is Arabic, from Egypt. It is an identity shaped for an idea. It is not merely a brand nor is it confined to its opening act. Our tailored men’s outfit - takm (outfit) mazbūt - made to order in our Cairo studio, is only a beginning.
I was commissioned by the brand to do their debut campaign which was shot enterily on location in Alexandria in some of my favorite cafes and locations using a mix of 35mm and 120 cameras and shooting only on E100 positive film to create the vibrant colors of films from 70s and 80s
